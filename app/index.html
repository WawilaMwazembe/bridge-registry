Built for work in the field

The same fields as the official List of Bridges template, captured once and checked before they reach the database.

Works offline

Record bridges with no signal. Data stays safely on the tablet until a connection returns.

GPS at the site

One tap captures latitude and longitude from the tablet, with accuracy shown.

Validated entries

Dropdown lists, bridge number format and range checks stop errors at the source.

Safe synchronisation

If two people edit the same bridge, nothing is overwritten. The inspector chooses which version to keep.

Verification workflow

Administrators check and approve records. Any later edit sends a bridge back for checking.

Full audit history

Every change is kept: who changed it, when, and from which device. Export to Excel at any time.

How it works

From the riverbank to the national inventory in four steps.

Capture on site

Open a bridge or register a new one, then take GPS and record condition.

Sync when online

Changes upload automatically as soon as the tablet has a connection.

Check & verify

Office staff review the records and mark them as verified.

Report

Export the list in the standard template or query it for planning.
